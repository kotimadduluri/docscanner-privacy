Doc Scanner privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

Doc Scanner ("the app") scans paper, finds it again by a word inside it, and exports PDFs. This page says what the app does with your information. The short version: your documents stay on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your scans, the text the app reads from them, your document titles, folder names and file names, and your settings are stored on your phone only. The app does not upload them, and we have no server to receive them. We cannot see them and cannot recover them if you delete them or lose your phone. When you share or save a PDF or image, it goes where you choose, to the app or folder you pick.

No accounts, no analytics of our own

There is no sign-up or sign-in. We do not run analytics or tracking SDKs of our own, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

The app reads text and (on Android) scans pages with Google's ML Kit. The recognition itself happens on your phone, and your page images and recognized text are not sent anywhere. Separately, ML Kit sends Google anonymous usage and diagnostic data so Google can run and improve ML Kit:

  • What: your device model and Android version and build, your country and language settings, the network type, the app's package name and version, ML Kit version and settings, performance and error counts, and a random install ID that ML Kit creates for this installation.
  • Never: your page images, the text read from them, your document titles or file names.
  • To whom: Google, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, in small batches (about 1 to 2 KB) after you use scanning or text recognition and when the phone has a network connection.
  • Why: diagnostics and analytics for ML Kit.
  • Choice: ML Kit does not offer a way to turn this off, so the app cannot offer one either. Deleting the app removes the install ID from your phone.

The scanner screen on Android is provided by Google Play services, which handles its own data under Google's terms. Google describes what ML Kit collects at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and its privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Camera and photos

The app asks for no camera permission on Android: the scanner screen comes from Google Play services and shows the camera to you directly. The pictures it returns, and any photos you choose to import with the system photo picker, are copied into the app's own private storage on your phone. The app reads only the photos you pick. If you choose Save to gallery, it adds your exported images to your gallery, and apps with photo access, and your gallery's own backup, can then see them.

Phone backups and deleting your data

The app is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer, so your documents are not copied by them. If you delete the app or lose the phone, your documents go with it unless you exported them. To delete everything the app holds, delete the app. Inside the app, deleting a document moves it to Trash, and it is removed for good after 30 days or when you empty Trash.

Children

Doc Scanner is for people 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will change the effective date above, and we will say what changed in the app's release notes.
